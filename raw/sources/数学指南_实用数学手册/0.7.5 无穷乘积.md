进一步的几个例子

$$
\prod_ {k = 2} ^ {\infty} \left(1 - \frac {1}{k ^ {2}}\right) = \frac {1}{2}, \qquad \prod_ {k = 0} ^ {\infty} \left(1 + x ^ {2 ^ {k}}\right) = \frac {1}{1 - x} \qquad (x \in \mathbb {C}, | x | <   1),
$$

$$
\sqrt {\frac {1}{2}} \sqrt {\frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \sqrt {\frac {1}{2}}} \sqrt {\frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \sqrt {\frac {1}{2}}} \dots = \frac {2}{\pi} \quad (\text {韦达,} 1 5 7 9),
$$

$$
\prod_ {k = 1} ^ {\infty} \left(1 - \frac {1}{(2 k) ^ {2}}\right) = \frac {2}{\pi}, \qquad \prod_ {k = 1} ^ {\infty} \left(1 - \frac {1}{(2 k + 1) ^ {2}}\right) = \frac {\pi}{4},
$$

$$
\left(\frac {2}{1}\right) \left(\frac {4}{3}\right) ^ {1 / 2} \left(\frac {6 \cdot 8}{5 \cdot 7}\right) ^ {1 / 4} \left(\frac {1 0 \cdot 1 2 \cdot 1 4 \cdot 1 6}{9 \cdot 1 1 \cdot 1 3 \cdot 1 5}\right) ^ {1 / 8} \dots = \mathrm{e},
$$

$$
\prod_ {k = 1} ^ {\infty} \frac {\sqrt [ k ]{\mathrm{e}}}{1 + 1 / k} = \mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{C}} \qquad (\text {欧拉常数} \mathrm{C} = 0. 5 7 7 2 1 5 \dots).
$$
