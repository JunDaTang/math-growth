例：

$$
(1) \Gamma (- 0. 2) = \frac {\Gamma (0 . 8)}{- 0 . 2} = - \frac {\Gamma (1 . 8)}{0 . 2 \cdot 0 . 8} = - \frac {0 . 9 3 1 3 8}{0 . 1 6} = - 5. 8 2 1 1 3.
$$

$$
(2) \Gamma (3. 2) = 2. 2 \cdot \Gamma (2. 2) = 2. 2 \cdot 1. 2 \cdot 2. \Gamma (1. 2) = 2. 2 \cdot 1. 2 \cdot 0. 9 1 8   1 7 = 2. 4 2 3   9 7.
$$
