##### 0.10.1 傅里叶变换

表中符号的说明：

C: 欧拉常数 (C = 0.57721567 $\cdots$ )

$$
\Gamma (z) = \int_ {0} ^ {\infty} \mathrm{e} ^ {- t} t ^ {z - 1} \mathrm{d} t, \quad \mathrm{Re} z > 0
$$

(Γ函数),

$$
J _ {\nu} (z) = \sum_ {n = 0} ^ {\infty} \frac {(- 1) ^ {n} \left(\frac {1}{2} z\right) ^ {\nu + 2 n}}{n ! \Gamma (\nu + n + 1)}
$$

(贝塞尔函数),

$$
\begin{array}{r l} {{K _ {\nu} (z) = \frac {1}{2} \pi (\sin (\pi \nu)) ^ {- 1} [ I _ {- \nu} (z) - I _ {\nu} (z) ]}} & {{\text {且}}} \\ {{I _ {\nu} (z) = \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{i} \pi \nu} J _ {\nu} (z \mathrm{e} ^ {\frac {1}{2} \mathrm{i} \pi})}} \end{array}
$$

(改进的贝塞尔函数),

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} C (x) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} \int_ {0} ^ {x} \frac {\cos t}{\sqrt {t}} \mathrm{d} t, \\ S (x) = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2 \pi}} \int_ {0} ^ {x} \frac {\sin t}{\sqrt {t}} \mathrm{d} t \end{array} \right.
$$

(菲涅耳积分),

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \mathrm{Si} (x) = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \frac {\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d} t, \\ \mathrm{si} (x) = - \int_ {x} ^ {\infty} \frac {\sin t}{t} \mathrm{d} t = \mathrm{Si} (x) - \frac {\pi}{2} \end{array} \right.
$$

(椭圆正弦),

$$
\operatorname{Ci} (x) = - \int_ {x} ^ {\infty} \frac {\cos t}{t} \mathrm{d} t
$$

(椭圆余弦).

我们有时也用记号 $\exp (x)$ 表示 $\mathrm{e}^x$ 。另外, $[x]$ 表示高斯括号, 它是小于等于 $x$ 的最大整数 $n$ .
