# 雅可比行列式

## 定义

假设 $N = K$（即映射 $f: M \subseteq \mathbb{R}^N \to \mathbb{R}^N$ 的维数相同），那么矩阵 $\boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p})$ 的行列式 $\det \boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p})$ 称为 $\boldsymbol{f}$ 的**雅可比（函数）行列式**，记作

```latex
\frac{\partial(f_1, \cdots, f_N)}{\partial(x_1, \cdots, x_N)} = \det \boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p}).
```

其中 $\boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p})$ 是 [[雅可比矩阵]]。

## 二维写法

本源例 2 给出 $N = K = 2$ 时的展开：

```latex
\frac{\partial(f_1, f_2)}{\partial(x_1, x_2)} = \det \boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p}) = \begin{vmatrix}
\partial_1 f_1(\boldsymbol{p}) & \partial_2 f_1(\boldsymbol{p}) \\
\partial_1 f_2(\boldsymbol{p}) & \partial_2 f_2(\boldsymbol{p})
\end{vmatrix},
```

```latex
\det \boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p}) = \partial_1 f_1(\boldsymbol{p}) \partial_2 f_2(\boldsymbol{p}) - \partial_2 f_1(\boldsymbol{p}) \cdot \partial_1 f_2(\boldsymbol{p}).
```

对线性映射 $f_1 = ax_1 + bx_2$、$f_2 = cx_1 + dx_2$，有

```latex
\det \boldsymbol{f}'(\boldsymbol{p}) = \begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{vmatrix} = ad - bc.
```
