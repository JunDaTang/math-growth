# 变分算子

**变分算子**指《数学指南——实用数学手册》1.5.8 节中反复出现的一阶微分算子

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{N} h_{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{k}} = h_1 \partial_1 + \dots + h_N \partial_N,
$$

其中 $\partial_j := \partial/\partial x_j$。该算子作用于函数时给出 $\pmb h$ 方向的 [[方向导数]]，而其 $r$ 次幂给出 $r$ 阶 [[n阶变分]]：

$$
\delta^{r} f(\boldsymbol{p};\boldsymbol{h}) = \left(\sum_{k=1}^{N} h_{k} \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{k}}\right)^{r} f(\boldsymbol{p}), \quad r = 1, \dots, n.
$$

## 二元的显式展开

当 $N = n = 2$ 时，

$$
\left(h_{1} \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{1}} + h_{2} \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{2}}\right)^{2} = h_{1}^{2} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{1}^{2}} + 2 h_{1} h_{2} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{1} \partial x_{2}} + h_{2}^{2} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{2}^{2}}.
$$

相应地

$$
\delta^{2} f(\boldsymbol{p};\boldsymbol{h}) = h_{1}^{2} \partial_{1}^{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}) + 2 h_{1} h_{2} \partial_{1} \partial_{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}) + h_{2}^{2} \partial_{2}^{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}).
$$

展开式的组合系数与二项式系数同构；将 $\partial_1 \partial_2$ 与 $\partial_2 \partial_1$ 合并为系数 $2$ 依赖于 [[施瓦茨定理]]（混合偏导次序可交换）。
