# 例：二元变分算子的平方展开

## 结果

当 $N = n = 2$ 时，《数学指南——实用数学手册》1.5.8 节给出 [[变分算子]] 平方的显式展开：

$$
\left(h_{1} \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{1}} + h_{2} \frac{\partial}{\partial x_{2}}\right)^{2} = h_{1}^{2} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{1}^{2}} + 2 h_{1} h_{2} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{1} \partial x_{2}} + h_{2}^{2} \frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial x_{2}^{2}}.
$$

用简写符号 $\partial_j := \partial/\partial x_j$，相应的变分为

$$
\delta f(\boldsymbol{p};\boldsymbol{h}) = h_{1} \partial_{1} f(\boldsymbol{p}) + h_{2} \partial_{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}),
$$

$$
\delta^{2} f(\boldsymbol{p};\boldsymbol{h}) = h_{1}^{2} \partial_{1}^{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}) + 2 h_{1} h_{2} \partial_{1} \partial_{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}) + h_{2}^{2} \partial_{2}^{2} f(\boldsymbol{p}).
$$

## 说明

展开式的组合系数与二项式系数同构；交叉项系数为 $2$ 依赖于混合偏导可交换（[[施瓦茨定理]]）。

## 证据类型与强度

来源给出的验证性例子，置信度高。
