**涉及内容**：

```latex
= \frac{\partial(f_1, \cdots, f_n)}{\partial(v_1, \cdots, v_n)}
\cdot \frac{\partial(v_1, \cdots, v_n)}{\partial(u_1, \cdots, u_n)}.
```
